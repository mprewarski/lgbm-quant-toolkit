How to read this

RMSE, Spearman, Pearson: prediction vs TARGET over all holdout rows pooled (P123 Lift tab).
IC/date: mean of the per-date Spearman correlations.
Avg%: mean weekly return x 52, after slippage (P123 "Average").
Cmp%: compounded annual return (P123 "Compounded").
SR: Avg% / annualized standard deviation. SD: annualized standard deviation.
Turn%: annualized one-way turnover of the bucket portfolio.
H-L: High minus Low, from the weekly return difference.
Tgt H-L: mean TARGET of the High bucket minus the Low bucket (TARGET units).
Univ: equal-weighted average of all stocks, no slippage.
Ensemble: the runs' predictions averaged, then scored once.
